How long does delivery take?+

Lagos orders placed before 2pm (Mon – Sat) are dispatched the same day and usually arrive same or next day. Other states take 2 – 5 working days with tracking.

Can I pay on delivery?+

Yes — pay on delivery (cash or transfer) is available for every Lagos address. Outside Lagos, pay securely online with card, bank transfer or USSD via Paystack.

What if my size doesn't fit?+

We'll exchange your size for free within 7 days of delivery, as long as the pair is unworn and in its original packaging. Just message us on WhatsApp.

How do I find my size?+

Our styles fit true to EU size. Measure your foot in cm and check our size guide. Between sizes? Go up half a size — or a full size for kids.

How much is delivery?+

Lagos express is ₦3,000 and nationwide delivery is ₦5,500 — free on orders over ₦100,000. You'll see the exact fee at checkout before you pay.

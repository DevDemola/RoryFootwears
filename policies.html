Delivery is free on orders over ₦50,000. You'll always see the exact fee at checkout before you pay. Once your order ships we'll send updates on WhatsApp.

Returns & exchanges

Wrong size? We'll swap it free within 7 days of delivery.

  • Items must be unworn, clean and in their original packaging.
  • Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and the size you need.
  • Exchanges depend on stock. If your size isn't available, we'll offer another style or store credit.
  • Sale items can be exchanged for size only.

Payment

Pay securely online with card (Visa, Mastercard, Verve), bank transfer or USSD through Paystack. Rory Footwear never sees or stores your card details.

Pay on delivery (cash or transfer) is available for Lagos addresses.

Privacy

We only collect what we need to process and deliver your order: your name, phone number, email and delivery address. We use it to fulfil orders, send order updates and, if you opt in, share new arrivals and offers. We never sell your data.

To update or delete your information, message us on WhatsApp.

Terms

  • Prices are in Naira (₦) and may change without notice; the price at checkout is the price you pay.
  • Orders are confirmed once payment is received or, for pay on delivery, once we confirm with you by phone or WhatsApp.
  • Product colours may vary slightly from photos due to screen settings.
  • Discount codes can't be combined and may expire.
